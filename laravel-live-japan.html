Laravel Live Japan 2026: Mount Fuji, one pixel at a time.

1 min read

Client:
Laravel
Role:
Design lead

The visual identity for Laravel Live Japan 2026 at Tachikawa Stage Garden in Tokyo, built around a pixel-drawn Mount Fuji.

Laravel Live Japan is the official Laravel community conference in Japan, and the first in East Asia. On May 26–27, 2026, more than 550 developers from over 40 countries gathered in Tokyo for two days of talks, hands-on learning, and community. The goal was to bridge the Japanese PHP community and the international Laravel community.

The identity pairs Laravel’s type with a pixel illustration, so it feels at home in Tokyo and is still unmistakably Laravel. Japanese and English sit side by side everywhere, just as the event ran in both languages.

The idea

A stamp from Tokyo, drawn one pixel at a time.

Mount Fuji, the sun, and the clouds are built on a pixel grid, the same grid behind every screen developers work in. The starting point was a vintage Japanese stamp: I kept its framing and its souvenir feel, then redrew it in bits and dots, so the illustration reads as vintage meeting digital.

Around it, the system stays simple: Laravel’s type set in solid blocks, the event name in English, and the venue in Japanese, all on Laravel red.

From the main stage to the tote bag, the same Fuji.

The illustration carried through the stage screens, the podium, crew and attendee shirts, totes, posters, and sponsor banners. Attendees shared more than 1,000 posts about the event on X, and kept the conversations going long after it ended.
