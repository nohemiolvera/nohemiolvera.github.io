Hacktoberfest 2024: Every contribution adds a tile.

1 min read

Client:
DigitalOcean
Role:
[Designer]
Project:
Identity and campaign

The 2024 identity for Hacktoberfest, DigitalOcean’s month-long celebration of open source, built on one idea: every contribution adds a tile to a larger mosaic.

Hacktoberfest has grown from 676 participants in 2014 to nearly 90,000 in 2023. The identity changes every year, so this one needed to feel fresh, different from last year’s, and not too attached to the DigitalOcean brand. It was a chance to create an identity outside DigitalOcean’s brand system.

A single pull request is small. Thousands of them, from people everywhere, build something none of them could make alone. The identity needed to show both at once.

The logo

Like ASCII art, the mark is built one character at a time.

The 11 marks Hacktoberfest’s eleventh year. It’s drawn on a pixel grid and built from an H and the 11, so the mark itself is made of smaller pieces coming together.

It’s a reflection of unity and progress, symbolizing how individual contributions come together to create something greater.

The system

Dark green, bright green, and a jolt of pink.

The palette pairs deep and bright greens with two pinks, so the mark can sit on any of them and still read. Monospaced, prompt-style headlines like “>events all month_long” keep it rooted in the terminal.

The campaign

One system, from the website to every story.

The identity ran through the Hacktoberfest website, the events pages, and a month of social posts and stories counting down to October.
