About Instagram

Nohemí has spent more than 10 years designing for companies that build software, from AlphaSights in New York to StackOverflow, Tighten, DigitalOcean, and Laravel.

She works at both scales: the craft of a single piece and the system that keeps a whole brand consistent as it grows. She’s interested in systems thinking and how everything is connected. She believes good design is useful, memorable, and built to scale.

Murals she painted in NYC and Brooklyn

Making since four, building since eleven.

She started drawing at four, and at eleven her curiosity led her to teach herself HTML and CSS from a site called Lissa Explains It All. Making and building have gone hand in hand ever since: the same curiosity that pulled her into code keeps her hands on paint, clay, and paper.

Born in Monterrey, she has lived in Toronto and New York and is now based in Mexico City, where she paints murals, writes, and makes ceramics. Her murals explore the duality of life through color and shape, which suits a designer who spends her days balancing structure and expression.
